# Project context

- Unity version: 6000.5.10f1. The project uses Animancer and Final IK, alongside older boxing/physics experiments.
- The user's current gameplay rig is `AimStudyBoxer` in `Assets/UndisputedTest/Boxing`, not the legacy `Assets/Scripts/BoxerPunchController`. Its saved scene is `Assets/Plugins/RootMotion/FinalIK/_DEMOS/Aim IK/Aim Boxing.unity`.
- Preserve unsaved Unity scene edits and existing per-hand jab tuning. `Tools > Undisputed > Boxing > Upgrade Selected Aim Study Boxer` upgrades the selected scene instance in place; the older `Aim Study Boxer` build command recreates the rig.
- Animancer owns the stance, three masked punch regions, and three matching defense layers. Apply procedural corrections after animation and before AimIK. Imported punch clips contain `ExpectedImpact` and attack-window markers; prefer these over generic strike-time guesses.
- Control preference: left stick is always movement. R1 holds head defense; L1 holds body defense. Right stick leans in all directions while idle or guarding. L2/R2 prepare their respective hand while held and throw on release; a short tap stays uncharged. Holding continuously drains stamina after the tap grace, with a capped power bonus that fades under exhaustion. Guard input cancels preparation. Stick up selects a hook and down selects an uppercut. Preparation must stay before the contact window and release must resume the same selected clip without restarting it. Aim steering is bounded; it locks on release (or before impact in the optional press-to-punch mode). Preserve this mapping when adding opponents.
- Optional `LeftTriggerJab`/`RightTriggerJab` clips override the legacy jab slots for neutral L2/R2. Shared release-to-impact timing is the default; `UsePerMotionTiming` opts into motion-specific timing. The upgrade applies a 0.22-or-lower exhausted speed, disables combo acceleration, explicitly assigns `Refrences/Guard` poses, and wires `FootstepAudio` to the existing step/squeak recordings. Footstep audio reads Aim Study movement and uses quiet distance-driven shuffles when bone plants are unavailable.
- Two-man fights: `Tools > Undisputed > Boxing > Setup Fight With Selected Aim Study Boxer` clones the selected study boxer into an AI opponent (`AimStudyAI` drives it through `AIBoxerInput`), gives each fighter `BoxerHealth` zones, `AimStudyCombat` hit reactions, a `LegsAnimator` on idle glue, and a `AimStudyPhysics` PuppetMaster bridge. `Tools > Undisputed > Boxing > Validate Selected Aim Study Fight` checks the wiring. Both menus are Undo-aware and never save the scene.
- PuppetMaster targets a transform-only physics skeleton — never call `PuppetMaster.SetUp` (it mutates the animated hierarchy). Standing mapping is zero on hips/legs/feet so Legs Animator owns foot placement; full mapping only on knockdown via `AimStudyCombat.SetDown`. Keep `Animator.updateMode` on Normal.
- Boxer damage stays in the glove sensor (`PunchHitbox`): it must be armed inside the strike window, can land once per punch, cannot hit its owner or a downed fighter, and sweeps the travelled segment for fast shots. `BoxerHealth` applies head/body guard coverage and dispatches to `AimStudyCombat.ReceiveImpact`.

# Verification

- From the project root: `dotnet build "Assembly-CSharp-Editor.csproj" --verbosity quiet`. Build project references too; a clean workspace has no generated dependency DLLs. Unity-generated `.csproj` files are ignored and should not be hand-edited.
- Dependency-free timing/buffer checks, in PowerShell: `& "./Tests/PunchPlaybackChecks.ps1"`.
- Rig wiring and clip coverage: `Tools > Undisputed > Boxing > Validate Selected Aim Study Boxer` in Unity.
- Compilation and timing tests do not verify visual animation quality. Check alternating jabs, both hooks, uppercuts, body shots, recovery, and actual bag contact in Play mode.
